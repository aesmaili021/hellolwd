# HelloLWD print brief — flyer + poster

Give this file to Claude Design as the only brief. Design two print pieces for a live site. Do not invent a new brand.

## Job

Two pieces that send internationals in Leeuwarden to **hellolwd.com**.

1. **A5 flyer** — hand-out at NHL Stenden, intro weeks, canteen tables, notice boards.
2. **A3 poster** — corridor walls, studio doors, supermarket boards near campus.

Someone walking past should get it in three seconds: this is the Leeuwarden briefing in your language. Then they scan the QR.

## Product

**HelloLWD** is a student news desk in Leeuwarden. NHL Stenden students started it in summer 2026.

The site does three things:

- Short local news from Leeuwarden and Friesland (summaries only; every story links to Omrop Fryslân, Omroep Leeuwarden, LC.nl, NOS)
- This weekend’s nights (clubs, live music, student parties)
- A first-month guide (BSN, DigiD, bins, huisarts)

Languages: Dutch, English, Spanish, Farsi (RTL). No accounts. No comments. No paywall.

URL: `https://hellolwd.com`  
Email: `info@hellolwd.com`

Do not use `www.hellolwd.com` on print.

## Audience

Primary: international students at NHL Stenden who just arrived and cannot read a Dutch news site at speed.

Secondary: exchange students, Spanish- and Farsi-speaking residents, Dutch students who want a short city scan.

They see this on a pinboard between housing ads and pizza flyers. It has to look like a city paper, not a SaaS landing page.

## Formats

| Piece | Size | Sides | Read time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Flyer | A5, 148 × 210 mm | Double-sided | 20 seconds |
| Poster | A3, 297 × 420 mm | One side | 3 seconds |

Print specs for both:

- CMYK
- 3 mm bleed
- Keep type and QR 10 mm inside the trim
- Export PDF/X-4 plus a PNG preview at 150 dpi
- QR must scan at arm’s length on the poster and at table distance on the flyer. Quiet zone around the QR. Test it.

## Brand (locked)

Wordmark: **Hello** + **LWD**  
LWD is the city’s airport code. Set LWD in the cyan. Do not write “Hello Leeuwarden” as the logo.

Mark: a **pompeblêd** (Frisian water-lily leaf), one vein down the middle. Red on navy, or red on paper. Redraw it as a clean vector. Do not use a photo lily, a clover, or the full Frisian flag as decoration.

Palette:

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Navy / brand | `#0B3D5C` | Ground, wordmark, poster field |
| Cyan | `#01A0E2` | LWD, QR frame, small rules |
| Accent red | `#C8102E` | Pompeblêd, one sharp hit |
| Paper | `#FFFFFF` | Flyer ground, type on navy |
| Mist | `#F7FAFC` | Flyer second side if needed |
| Ice | `#E1F3FC` | Chips, language pills |
| Ink | `#374151` | Body on paper |
| Mute | `#8894A0` | Meta, URL |

Type on the site is a heavy grotesque, extra-bold, tight tracking on headlines. For print, pick a characterful display + a readable text face. No Inter, Roboto, Arial, Space Grotesk.

Tone: editorial, local, dry. Like a small-city desk, not a startup launch.

## Flyer structure

Front: navy or paper. Huge wordmark. Pompeblêd. Headline. Four language lines or four chips. QR. URL in plain type under the QR.

Back: the three bullets, the origin line, email, QR repeated smaller so the sheet still works if someone keeps only one side.

Keep a lot of air. This is not a timetable.

## Poster structure

One field. Wordmark you can read from the corridor. One headline. Language chips. QR bottom-right (LTR) or bottom-left if you flip for a Farsi-led variant.

No paragraph on the poster. No list of features. No screenshot of the website.

## Visual direction

Commit to one idea: **a Frisian city desk on paper**.

- Heavy type, tight headlines, one red leaf
- Cyan as a signal, not a gradient wash
- If you use a photo, make it Leeuwarden and specific: Oldehove, Zaailand, Rengerslaan, a wet evening street. No stock “diverse students laughing with laptops”
- Texture is fine (uncoated stock, light grain). Purple blobs, glassmorphism, and floating 3D icons are not
- You may set a second poster that is paper-on-paper (white, navy type, red leaf) for bright hallways

## Do not

- Write HelloLWD as Hello LWD with a space in the wordmark
- Use www
- Put KVK, a fake address, or “Street 00”
- Add Instagram / TikTok unless we give you handles later
- Promise apps, accounts, or a newsletter
- Cover the sheet in flags
- Generate a new mascot
- Use the grocery-deals idea; that page is not live
